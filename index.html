About

Emflo Advisory is a boutique product strategy, UX and data analytics consultancy, founded in 2017 by Mark Flowers and based in Los Gatos, California.

Mark is a software veteran, data nerd, procurement practitioner and global business leader based in the San Francisco Bay Area. His work brings together software, data and analytics, and hands-on procurement and operations, and now AI. He is a Certified Sourcing Professional (CSP) and a Lean Six Sigma Green Belt, studied at Griffith University in Australia, and speaks Japanese.

We work with founders, executives and leadership teams who want technology and data to drive the business, and who value straight answers.
